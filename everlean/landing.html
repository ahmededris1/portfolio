Do frontline workers need an account or email?

No. Shared tablets and QR codes on the line allow anonymous or name-only submission. Accounts are optional and can use personnel numbers instead of email.

How long does it take to get started?

Most teams submit their first ideas the same day. There is no IT installation; for Enterprise SSO we plan one to two weeks.

Can we map our existing idea process and bonus scheme?

Yes. Statuses, evaluation criteria, approval steps and reward rules are configurable, and Enterprise maps your full innovation process.

Is everlean compliant with works council requirements?

We provide a DPA, role concept and works-council information pack. Performance evaluation of individuals can be switched off entirely.

What happens to our data if we cancel?

You can export everything to Excel or CSV at any time. After cancellation, data is deleted within 30 days.
