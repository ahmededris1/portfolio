01The problem

Great ideas were dying in a desktop form.

everlean helps plants collect improvement ideas and safety observations from every employee. The concept worked. The experience did not: built for office desks, used on noisy shop floors.

How research changed the design

01
Gloves, noise and 3-minute breaks.

Typing is the barrier, not motivation. Ideas happen at the machine.

→
DecisionVoice-first capture with Lean Copilot, 56 pt targets, QR codes that pre-fill the location.
02
No feedback means no second idea.

72% never learned what happened to their suggestion.

→
DecisionParcel-tracking timeline for every idea, push updates and “time to decision” as a headline KPI.
03
Managers live in the 9:00 meeting.

The board runs on a TV in front of 15 people standing.

→
DecisionBoard-first portal: drag and drop, next task on each card, detail as a side drawer that keeps context.
04
31% are not native German speakers.

People held back because they worried about spelling.

→
DecisionSpeak in any language, Copilot translates. UI fully in German and English.
05
CI has to prove its money.

Programs were cut when nobody could show the savings.

→
DecisionSavings tracked per idea, a cockpit for management and an ROI calculator on the website.
06
Night shift, dim halls, bright screens.

Tablets on the line were turned down to unreadable.

→
DecisionFull dark mode from day one, driven by FDS Light/Dark tokens rather than a separate design.
03Process

Every decision had to survive the same five gates.

A lightweight decision framework kept a fast team honest: frame the problem, generate options, score them against evidence, test the winner, then log it.

Weeks 1–3Discover

Shadowing, interviews, analytics, baseline usability test.

Weeks 4–5Define

Insights, “How might we” questions, success metrics with PM.

Weeks 6–9Design

Concepts, decision matrices, design system, high-fidelity flows.

Weeks 10–11Validate

Prototype tests on the line, 3 rounds, 18 participants.

Week 12Hand-off

Tokens, components, specs, decision log for engineering.

Decision: how should a worker capture an idea?

Scored 1–5 against research, weighted. Tested with 8 operators before committing.

Weights: user impact 40% · business 25% · effort 20% · risk 15%
OptionUser impactBusinessEffortRiskScore
A · Shorter form (5 → 3 steps)2.534.54.53.4
B · Photo now, details later3.52.53.52.53.1
C · Voice-first with AI draft54.52.534.1

Why C won: in tests, 7 of 8 operators finished a voice submission in under a minute, against 3 of 8 with the short form. Typing stays available as a fallback, and every AI draft is reviewed by the person before it is sent.

08Light & dark

One design, two modes, zero extra screens.

Every color is a semantic FDS token with a Light and a Dark value. Switching mode re-maps the tokens, so the whole product turns dark without a single redrawn screen. Try it.

11Learnings

What I would keep, and what I would change.

Go to the line on day oneThree hours of shadowing changed more decisions than three weeks of desk research.
Tokens are a product decisionBuilding on semantic tokens made dark mode, white-labelling and the app nearly free.
AI needs a human gateCopilot drafts, people decide. That single rule won over the works council.
